Scroller

Small buttons that scroll any page in Safari. At the end of a chapter, it goes to the next chapter by itself on most sites.

  1. Copy the bookmark.
  2. In Safari, open any page. Tap Share, then Add to Favorites.
  3. Touch and hold the new bookmark in the Favorites bar. Tap Edit. Name it Scroller. Delete the address and paste.
  4. Open a manga page. Tap Scroller in the Favorites bar. It starts playing at once. Tap it again to remove the buttons.

No Favorites bar? Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari. Turn on Show Favorites Bar.

To test, open in Safari and tap Scroller.
